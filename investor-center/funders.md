# Funders

**This file is the source of truth for the `investor-center` skill.** Content goes here; the skill
reads it and never invents anything beyond it.

Keep entries short and factual. Anything you are unsure of, mark `unverified` — the skill will pass
that uncertainty on rather than presenting it as fact.

---

## Venture

Use one block per fund. Delete the example.

### Example Capital
- **stage:** pre-seed, seed
- **cheque:** $500k–$2M
- **leads:** yes
- **sectors:** B2B SaaS, healthcare IT
- **geography:** US, remote-friendly
- **thesis:** *"quote their own words where possible — it is what makes the match checkable"*
- **warm paths:** who we know there, and through whom
- **notes:** anything specific — a partner who covers our space, a portfolio conflict, how they
  prefer to be approached
- **last verified:** YYYY-MM-DD

---

## Angels

Same shape, plus:
- **operator background:** what they actually did — this is usually why you want them
- **typical cheque:** angels vary enormously
- **value beyond money:** intros, domain credibility, a specific customer relationship

---

## Grants and non-dilutive

⚠ **Deadlines are the critical field here.** An expired grant is not a recommendation.

### Example Grant Programme
- **funder:** the body running it
- **amount:** and whether it is a grant, a loan, or matched funding
- **deadline:** YYYY-MM-DD — **and whether it recurs**
- **eligibility:** stage, geography, sector, incorporation type, spend restrictions
- **dilution:** none / warrant / equity option
- **effort:** rough — a two-week application for $25k is a real trade-off
- **notes:** what they actually fund, and what they reject
- **last verified:** YYYY-MM-DD

---

## Accelerators

- **programme, batch timing, terms** (cheque and equity), **location / remote**
- **what it is actually good for** — some are capital, some are network, some are credibility
- **deadline** and **last verified**

## Passed / not a fit

Keep them, with the reason. **This section saves more time than the others** — it stops the same
names being revisited every few weeks, and three passes citing the same gap is the most useful
signal in a raise.

- **Fund** — passed YYYY-MM-DD — *"their stated reason, verbatim"*
