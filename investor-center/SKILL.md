# Investor center

**The aim:** the founder stops guessing who to talk to. Fit is checkable — stage, sector, cheque
size, geography, whether they lead — and most wasted fundraising effort is spent on funders who were
never going to be a fit.

## Where the data lives

`funders.md` in this skill directory. **That file is the source of truth.** Never invent a fund, a
partner, a cheque size or a deadline — if it is not in the file, say it is not in the file. A
plausible-sounding fund that does not exist costs the founder a week.

If the file is empty or thin, say so plainly and answer from what is there. Do not fill gaps from
memory; funding data goes stale fast and a wrong stage or cheque size sends the founder into a
meeting that was never going to work.

## How to match

Read the company's stage, sector, geography and raise size from memory before recommending anyone.
Then rank by **fit, not fame**:

1. **Stage** — the hardest filter. A seed fund at Series A is a waste of both sides' time.
2. **Cheque size against the round** — someone writing $250k cannot lead a $3M round.
3. **Sector and thesis** — quote their stated thesis where the file has it.
4. **Leads or follows** — a round with no lead is not a round.
5. **Warm path** — a route in beats a better-fitting fund with no route.

## How to report

For each: **name, why they fit, what the route in is, and the single reason they might pass.** That
last one is the most useful line and the one usually left out.

Group by tier — approach now / approach once we have X / not a fit and why. **"Not a fit and why" is
as valuable as the shortlist**, because it stops the founder revisiting the same names in three
weeks.

## Non-dilutive

Grants, accelerators and programmes are in the same file, marked as such. Surface them whenever they
apply — founders systematically forget non-dilutive money exists, and it does not cost equity.

⚠ **Deadlines matter more than fit here.** A perfect grant whose deadline passed last week is not a
recommendation. Check the date before suggesting one, and say when it closes.

## What you never do

- **Never contact a fund.** Draft the outreach, show it, wait.
- **Never claim a relationship the file does not record.**
- **Never state a cheque size, thesis or deadline that is not in the file.**
- Say *"the list does not cover this"* rather than reaching for general knowledge. Being narrow and
  right beats being broad and wrong.
