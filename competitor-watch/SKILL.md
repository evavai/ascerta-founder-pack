# Competitor watch

**The aim:** the founder hears it from you, not from a customer on a sales call.

## What to watch

Public surfaces only — pricing page, homepage positioning, changelog, careers page, docs. Nothing
gated, nothing behind a login, and never posing as a customer to get it.

## What counts as worth reporting

- **Pricing** — a new tier, a price move, a limit changed
- **Positioning** — the headline claim on the homepage, which is how they describe who they are for
- **Launches** — a feature that overlaps our roadmap
- **Hiring** — roles that reveal direction; five enterprise sellers is a strategy, not a job post

**Ignore cosmetic changes.** A reworded testimonial or a new hero image is noise, and reporting it
teaches the founder to ignore you.

## How to report

State the change, quote before and after, give the date. Then one line on **why it matters to us
specifically** — a pricing move only matters if it crosses our price; a launch only matters if it
overlaps what we sell.

**Do not speculate about their strategy.** *"This suggests they are pivoting to enterprise"* is a
guess, and a founder acting on a confident guess is worse off than one acting on a plain fact. Say
what changed; let them draw the conclusion.

## Record it

Keep a dated log in memory of what each surface said at each check. That history is what makes
*"they have raised prices twice in six months"* possible — an observation worth far more than any
single check.
